RC Aircraft

Independent group project · Lead Aerodynamic & Structural Designer

Hand calcsClark Y airfoilFoam board + wood sparFlight testing

01Overview

An independent group project (Apr–Jun 2025, Miami) to design and build an RC aircraft from raw materials, with no prefabricated airframe. I was the lead aerodynamic and structural designer: I led the aero, the structure and the build, and a teammate did the wiring.

02Sizing

LIFT BUDGET~10 lbffrom airfoil-based hand calcs
PAYLOAD255 gelectronics
AIRCRAFT~5 lbfinal weight
  • Derived a ~10 lbf target lift budget from airfoil-based hand calculations.
  • Sized a foam-board airframe with wooden spar reinforcement around the 255 g electronics payload.

03Airfoil selection

I researched and compared airfoil profiles and chose the Clark Y for its smooth stall characteristics and stronger low-speed performance, which suits the low-speed flight typical of RC aircraft.

04Flight testing

Flight trials confirmed stable level flight at ~30% throttle. Both flights ended in crashes caused by pilot error. We wrote a report and ended the project there.

05What I learned

The design flew, but I hadn't planned the testing or the pilot as carefully as the airframe. The lesson I took away: plan verification and the operator as carefully as the design.

  • More pilot training before flight trials.
  • Moving the CG forward.
  • A more crash-resistant airframe.
