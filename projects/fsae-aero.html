FSAE Aero: CFD & Composites

HyTech Racing · Rear wing CFD sweep and carbon fiber aero parts

ANSYS FluentPACE HPCCFDResin infusionMold tooling

01Overview

On HyTech Racing I support the aero side of the car in two ways: CFD on the rear wing, and building the carbon fiber parts. This page covers both.

02Rear wing angle sweep

  • Ran a 5-case CFD sweep of rear wing angles in ANSYS Fluent on Georgia Tech's PACE high-performance computing cluster.
  • This gave the team its first medium-downforce CFD dataset, alongside the existing high-downforce data.
  • Identified a wing angle that cut drag by 28% for a 17% downforce trade-off versus the high-downforce baseline, and presented the findings to the aero lead to inform configuration selection for high-speed corners.

03Getting results I could trust

Before relying on the numbers, I reduced CFD solve residuals by 90% by tuning body-of-influence sizing and iteration count, smoothing mesh gradients and improving flux conservation across the model.

I also worked with my lead to balance the wing setup against the powertrain team's radiator cooling-flow needs.

04Composite fabrication

I fabricate structural composite aero parts with resin-infused carbon fiber layups: precision mold tooling, layup, debulking, sanding and surface finishing.
