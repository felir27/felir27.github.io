PROJECT DETAIL

JNCY Design for Casting

JNCY Jewelers · 3D CAD Design Intern · Summers 2023 & 2024

Rhino 3DSubD / NURBSInvestment casting3D resin printingDFM

01Overview

Over two summers (2023 and 2024) I worked as a 3D CAD Design Intern at JNCY Jewelers in Miami, designing 12+ custom CAD models in Rhino 3D (SubD and NURBS) that went to 3D resin printing and lost-resin investment casting. The work generated thousands of dollars in revenue.

02Designing backward from the process

  • Worked backward from lost-resin casting constraints so each design met both the client's needs and what the shop could actually make.
  • Prepared watertight models for 3D resin printing and investment casting, resolving wall thickness and shrinkage constraints.
  • Produced renders for the manufacturing handoff.

03Working with clients

I worked directly with the company's leadership and with clients to turn conceptual sketches into precise digital models within tight manufacturing tolerances.
