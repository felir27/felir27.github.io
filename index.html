About

Bio

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Georgia Tech, originally from Miami. What I enjoy most is mechanical design and manufacturing: making parts that can actually be built and bolted together.

On HyTech Racing I work across suspension and aero. I built the MATLAB tool that sets bolt torque specs for the suspension-to-monocoque joints on our new carbon fiber chassis, ran a CFD sweep of the rear wing, and fabricate carbon fiber aero parts. Before that, I designed casting-ready CAD models for a jewelry manufacturer, so I think about how a part gets made from the first sketch.

I'm looking for a Spring or Summer 2027 mechanical engineering internship in design or manufacturing, and I'm available starting January 2027.
